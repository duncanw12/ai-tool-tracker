AI for IT & Cybersecurity

24 tools. Prices and free tiers shown only where we checked them on the official site.

Breaking into IT? Start here

Cert study and labs: TryHackMe's Echo tutor and HTB Coach. Scripting and PowerShell: practice with GitHub Copilot or Warp, then scan your own scripts for free with Semgrep or Snyk. SOC and malware practice: stand up Wazuh, the free open-source SIEM, or Security Onion for network monitoring and threat hunting, in your home lab and point it at a few VMs, paste a suspicious PowerShell sample into VirusTotal Code Insight and build a small alert workflow on the Tines free edition. Help desk and MSP work: start the 30-day free trial of Atera, the RMM and ticketing platform many MSPs run, and practice working tickets and generating scripts with its built-in AI Copilot. For internal IT help desk roles, the Freshservice free trial lets you practice incidents, a knowledge base and a self-service portal. Vulnerability scanning: run Nuclei against your own lab VMs (never anything you don't own) and try its -ai option to turn a plain-English check into a template. AI security: point garak at a local model to see how prompt injection and jailbreaks actually work, then write your own red-team tests with promptfoo. Home lab: run local models with Ollama and turn notes into quizzes with Gemini Notebook. The SOC copilots further down (Security Copilot, Purple AI, Charlotte AI) are enterprise tools you'll likely meet on the job, so their docs are worth a read even before you can use them.
