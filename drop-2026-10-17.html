Pricing filled in today

These listings showed "See site" yesterday. We checked each official pricing page on Oct 17, 2026.

  • Lovable: Pro from $25/mo ($21/mo billed annually); free plan has 5 daily credits
  • ChatGPT: Plus $20/mo; lower-cost Go plan also available

Category and page changes

  • AI for IT & Cybersecurity grows from 15 to 18 tools. Its Breaking into IT guide now suggests standing up Wazuh in your home lab for SOC practice: real alerts, real logs, and a skill that shows up in SOC analyst job posts.
  • Gemini updated for the new Gemini agent for work (announced Oct 8), which runs inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Calendar.
  • A note on CyberStrikeAI: it can run real attack tools, so only point it at your own lab or systems you have written permission to test.

Checked, not listed

  • Cantina Apex Flash-1: an open-weights security research model released Oct 16. Interesting, but the only results so far are the vendor's own evaluation, so we're waiting for outside testing.
  • Chainguard Workspaces and Checks: secure sandboxes and CI for AI coding agents, announced Oct 16 as 30-day beta trials. We'll revisit when they're generally available.
  • ThreatBook RedAgents: an enterprise AI pentest aggregator launched Oct 16, trial by application only.
  • Jira Service Management Free: a good free help desk to practice ticketing on (up to 3 agents), but its Rovo AI features need a paid plan, so it doesn't fit an AI directory yet.
