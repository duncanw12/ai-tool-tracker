Why these three

  • OpenCode is one of the most-used coding agents right now: about 216,000 GitHub stars and, per its site, 16 million developers a month. It's free and MIT-licensed, and you can sign in with a GitHub Copilot or ChatGPT plan you already pay for.
  • Atera shows up in a lot of help desk and MSP job posts. As of June 2026 its AI Copilot (script generator, ticket summaries, KB drafts) is included in every plan, and the 30-day free trial needs no credit card.
  • garak is NVIDIA's open-source scanner for LLM apps, with about 9,600 GitHub stars. Version 0.17.1 shipped Oct 16. Only scan models and apps you own or have permission to test.

Category and page changes

  • AI for IT & Cybersecurity grows from 18 to 20 tools. Its Breaking into IT guide adds a help desk and MSP step (practice ticketing and scripting on the Atera trial) and an AI security step (run garak against a local model).
  • Unknown pages on the site now return a proper "page not found" instead of the home page.

Checked, not listed

  • Gemini CLI: Google stopped serving free and Google AI Pro/Ultra users in Gemini CLI on June 18, 2026 and moved them to the new Antigravity CLI. It still works with paid API keys and enterprise licenses, so we're watching Antigravity CLI instead.
  • OpenRouter Armor: a beta classifier that checks an AI agent's tool calls against your rules before they run. Free during the beta, pricing not announced yet. It's part of OpenRouter, which is already listed.
  • PilotDeck 2.0: an open-source (AGPL-3.0) agent OS from Tsinghua's THUNLP lab, released Oct 18. Too new to judge traction.
  • Safeguard on-prem runners: Safeguard's SAST scans can now run inside your own environment (Oct 17). An enterprise supply-chain product, so not a fit for most readers yet.
