Why these three

  • ServiceNow ITSM calls itself the world's #1 IT service management software, and it's the ticketing system behind a large share of help desk and service desk job posts at bigger employers. Its Now Assist AI summarizes incidents, suggests solutions and drafts responses, and the Advanced and Prime packages add AI agents. Pricing is quote-only, but ServiceNow's Developer Program gives every member one free Personal Developer Instance with admin access, which is yours to keep as long as you keep using it. Log a few incidents, build a catalog item and write a knowledge article, and you'll have something concrete to talk about in an interview.
  • GhidraMCP connects an AI assistant to Ghidra, the NSA's free reverse-engineering suite, through MCP. The assistant can decompile functions, list imports and exports and rename things as it figures them out, which makes it a patient tutor for malware analysis practice. It has about 11,400 GitHub stars, and v2.0 shipped Oct 10 for Ghidra 12.1.4. Only open samples inside an isolated VM, and check what the AI tells you against the disassembly.
  • Pocket TTS from Kyutai is open-source text-to-speech that runs on an ordinary CPU and clones a voice from a short clip. Version 4.0, out Oct 20, adds English models of 18.2M, 6.9M and 1.9M parameters (Kyutai marks the 1.9M ones experimental) and ready-made binaries, so you don't need Python. The code is MIT and the weights are CC-BY-4.0, though the voice cloning weights sit behind a free Hugging Face terms click-through. About 10,000 GitHub stars. Only clone voices you have permission to use.

Listing updates

Checked on Oct 21, 2026 against official pages and announcements.

  • Devin: devin.ai/pricing still returns a 404, but Devin's billing docs list a Free plan, Pro at $20 a month, Max at $200 a month and Teams from $80 a month (full seats $40 each). The listing now shows the full lineup.
  • DeepSeek open-sourced DeepInfer (Apache-2.0), the inference framework behind its own DeepSeek V4.1 Flash service. It's built for data-center hardware (NVIDIA SM100 GPUs and Huawei Ascend 950 NPUs) and ships offline inference only, so it's for infrastructure teams rather than home labs. It passed 16,000 GitHub stars in its first days.
