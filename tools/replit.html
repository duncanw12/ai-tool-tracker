Replit

Browser IDE with Replit Agent that builds and deploys apps from a prompt. New Oct 22, 2026: Monitoring Agent, an on-call agent for published apps. Turn on monitoring alerts and Auto-fix and it investigates production issues from your app's logs and metrics, attempts and validates a fix and emails you a summary; with the experimental Auto-republish setting it can also publish the fix. Replit hasn't published fix-accuracy numbers.
