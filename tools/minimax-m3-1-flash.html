MiniMax M3.1 Flash

MiniMax's open-weight multimodal mixture-of-experts model (about 428B total, 23B active per token, up to 1M tokens of context) for coding, agentic work, computer use and cybersecurity tasks. Takes text, image and video input. MiniMax's own benchmarks put it at 73.7% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 61.7 on OSWorld 2.0, close to big closed models on several agent tests. Too big for a home PC: MiniMax recommends serving it with SGLang, vLLM or TokenSpeed, or using it through MiniMax Agent and the MiniMax API. New in October 2026.
